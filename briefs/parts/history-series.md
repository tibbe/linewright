The pull request's commits land on the default branch as they are, so leave the commits the branch adds to it a coherent series: each makes one change, its message says what the change is and why, and none corrects another. Fold a correction into the commit it corrects, and reword a commit whose message no longer fits it. A change that corrects no commit, such as one the feedback asks for that the pull request didn't make yet, is a commit of its own. Keep the branch's base where it is. Keep each commit a person wrote a commit of its own, with its message: make a correction to one a commit of your own.
