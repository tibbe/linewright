To show a screenshot or a video in the Markdown your result posts, save the file in your attachments directory, `{{directory}}`, and refer to it by its absolute path there: as the destination of a Markdown image (`![what it shows](/path/to/file.png)`) or link, or as an HTML `img`'s `src`. Linewright uploads each file you refer to and puts its URL in place of the path. A video (`.mp4`, `.mov` or `.webm`) embedded alone in its paragraph plays inline; anywhere else it becomes a link to it.
